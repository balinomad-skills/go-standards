# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and commits in this repository.

## Expected conduct

- Keep discussion on the project: the skill files, scripts, and workflows.
- Disagree with the content, not the person.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Assume good intent.
- Accept correction and update your position when the evidence changes.

## Unacceptable conduct

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Spam, advertising, or trolling.
- Off-topic debate in issues and pull requests.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Repeated bad-faith argument after a decision is made.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately through GitHub's report abuse form at https://github.com/contact/report-abuse, or through the contact information on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not use public issues or pull requests for conduct reports.

## Enforcement

Complaints are reviewed promptly and fairly. Reporter privacy is respected. Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, reject contributions, and block users who violate this code. Enforcement decisions are final. Reasons are given when appropriate.
